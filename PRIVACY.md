# Bhakti Clock – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

Bhakti Clock plays music you choose at times you choose. It is built to work entirely on your phone.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** Bhakti Clock does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data.
It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not need an internet
connection to work.

Everything you set up stays on your phone:

- your schedules (times, days, names, volume)
- the audio files, music app or link you picked for each schedule
- the Bluetooth speakers you allowed

This information is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Permissions and why they are needed

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) | To start the music on the exact minute you set. |
| Run in the background / ignore battery optimisation | So Android does not stop the scheduled music. |
| Notifications | To show what is playing, with a Stop button. |
| Nearby devices (Bluetooth) | To list your paired speakers so you can choose which ones the music may play on. The app only reads speaker names; it does not scan for or connect to other devices. |
| Start at boot | To set your schedules again after the phone restarts. |
| Display over other apps, full-screen notification | To open your chosen music app at the scheduled time, even when the screen is off. |
| Notification access | To press Play in your chosen music app and check that the music started. The app only looks at which app is playing music. It does not read, store or share the content of your notifications. |
| Read audio files you pick | To play the files you select. The app can open only the files you choose. |

## Other apps and links

If you schedule a music app or a link (for example a YouTube Music link), that app opens and
plays as usual, and that app's own privacy policy applies to it.

## Children

Bhakti Clock is a general-audience utility and is not designed for children.
