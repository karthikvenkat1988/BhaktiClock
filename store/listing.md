## Full description
Start every morning and evening with the sound of prayer – automatically.

Bhakti Clock plays the devotional music you love at the times you choose, every day, through your Bluetooth speaker. Set Suprabhatam for 6 AM and Vishnu Sahasranamam for 6 PM once, and your home fills with them every day without you lifting a finger.

Made for a spare phone that stays connected to a speaker at home, so it works like a dedicated prayer clock.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Schedule music for any time, on the days you choose
• Play audio files from your phone: one track or a whole set, in order or shuffled
• Or open your favourite music app or a song or playlist link at the set time and start it playing
• Choose which speakers may play. The app waits up to 2 minutes for one of them and plays on the phone if none connects
• Set the volume for each schedule, so it never plays too soft or too loud
• Pause the music automatically after a set number of minutes
• Starts on the exact minute, even when the phone is asleep, and sets itself up again after a restart

A CALM, BEAUTIFUL APP
• A warm saffron design with a flickering diya and a live clock
• See at a glance what plays next and how long until it starts
• A Now Playing card with a Stop button

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

Perfect for daily slokas, bhajans, stotrams, Suprabhatam, Vishnu Sahasranamam, Hanuman Chalisa, Lalitha Sahasranamam and any music you want at a fixed time.
